Simple Process

How it works

Reporting an infrastructure hazard takes under two minutes. Follow these simple steps to notify the team.

1

Identify problem

Spot a damaged road, broken light, blocked drain, or safety hazard in your neighborhood or campus.

2

Open site

Visit the Lokrun platform on any smartphone, tablet, or laptop without needing to install an app.

3

Select issue type

Choose from 6 clear civic categories so the report is routed directly to the right municipal crew.

4

Enter details & location

Describe the issue and landmark, or tap "Detect my location" to tag exact GPS coordinates.

5

Attach photo

Optionally upload a clear image (up to 5 MB) to help technicians assess equipment and repairs needed.

6

Submit & get ID

Receive an instant tracking reference code to monitor verification and resolution status.

Scope of Service

What you can report

We categorize common civic infrastructure challenges across Chandwad and SNJB campus routes.

🚧

Potholes / damaged roads

Asphalt depressions, craters, missing pavers, road shoulder erosion, or sunken utility trenches.

Coverage: Craters, fissures, shoulder collapse
🌊

Open or blocked drains

Clogged stormwater channels, missing drain covers, silt accumulation, or foul sewage blockages.

Coverage: Culverts, missing slabs, silt blockages
🌧️

Waterlogging

Standing rainwater flooding carriageways, burst drinking pipes, or lack of roadside drainage gradient.

Coverage: Roadway ponding, pipeline bursts
💡

Broken streetlights

Unlit solar poles, flickering lamps, exposed junction box wires, or physical pole damage.

Coverage: Dark corridors, exposed junction boxes
🚦

Traffic-signal problems

Blacked-out blinkers, damaged signal housings, broken timing cycles, or bent traffic signage.

Coverage: Inactive warning blinkers, damaged poles
⚠️

Construction hazards / other

Unbarricaded excavation pits, dumped building debris, protruding rebar, or unlit roadside gravel heaps.

Coverage: Unfenced pits, unbarricaded roadworks
